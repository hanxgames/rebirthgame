HanX Games · ReBirth

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 (updated)

Summary: ReBirth uses Google Firebase on Android and, from version 2.2.0, also on iOS, to run online features (an anonymous account, the Weekly Event leaderboard, announcements) and to understand how the game is played and fix crashes. On iOS, version 2.1.2 and earlier have no account and no analytics. We (HanX Games) do not sell your data and have no servers of our own besides these Google services. If you play the Weekly Event (Premium), your display name and event results appear on a public leaderboard (see section 2). If you send a scenario to us with "Send to HanX" in the Scenario Workshop, it comes only to us; if we pick it, we publish it as an event (see section 2). The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes some device data to show and measure ads; on iOS, personalised ads are used only if you allow tracking. ReBirth Premium removes all ads. Your campaigns and settings stay on your device; if you connect Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), they are also backed up to your own Google or Apple cloud storage.

1. iOS and iPadOS (App Store)

From version 2.2.0, the iOS version uses the same online services as the Android version (section 2), together with Apple's Game Center and iCloud. Version 2.1.2 and earlier send no data to Firebase and have no account. The game also works offline.

  • Game Center (Apple), optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game recognises you automatically; otherwise you play as a guest with an anonymous account and can connect later in the game's Settings → "Connect Game Center". From Apple the game receives your Game Center player ID and display name (alias). They are used to link your anonymous Firebase account to Game Center (Firebase Authentication's Game Center sign-in, for which Apple's identity verification signature and your player ID are sent to Google) and as your name on the Weekly Event leaderboard. Game Center achievements and leaderboards are not used.
  • iCloud saves (Apple). While Game Center is connected, your progress (achievements, career statistics, best Crisis Desk and event results, tutorial status, up to four campaign save slots and your Sefer save) is backed up with Game Center saved games to your own iCloud Drive, so you can continue on another device with the same Apple Account. This data stays in your Apple Account; HanX Games cannot access it. If you are not signed in to iCloud or iCloud Drive is turned off, your progress stays on your device only. You can delete it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Firebase (Google). Analytics and Crashlytics, Authentication (an anonymous user ID), Realtime Database (gameplay statistics and the Weekly Event leaderboard), Remote Config and Cloud Messaging work as described in section 2, with the same data and retention.
  • Notifications. Return reminders are scheduled on your device and send nothing to a server. Announcements are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging through the Apple Push Notification service; for this, your device's notification token and topic subscriptions (all players, and your game language) are processed by Google and Apple. The game asks for permission with the system prompt; you can turn notifications off in the game's settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Advertising (free version only). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process your device's IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, ad interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data, and an advertising identifier.
  • Tracking and your choices. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise you still see ads, but they are not personalised by tracking across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in the game's Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Premium removes ads. After you buy ReBirth Premium, no ads are requested.
  • Data on your device. Saved campaigns, settings, achievements and a local record of your Premium purchase are stored inside the app's sandbox on your device (and, from version 2.2.0, backed up to iCloud as described above while Game Center is connected). Deleting the app deletes the copy on your device.
  • Purchases. ReBirth Premium is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Permissions. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. From version 2.2.0 it asks for permission to send notifications.

2. Android (Google Play)

The Android version (and, from version 2.2.0, the iOS version; see section 1) uses Google services to run online features and the free, ad-supported version. Through these services the following data may be processed:

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google): in-game events (for example, campaign started or finished, screens opened, buttons tapped, tutorial steps, frame rate) and crash reports, linked to a Firebase installation ID and your anonymous Firebase user ID. Firebase Analytics also records in-app purchase events (the product and its price, not your payment details). Used to understand how the game is played, improve it and fix crashes.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google): anonymous gameplay statistics linked to your anonymous user ID: the game mode and screen you are on, map and in-game day, game version, language and region setting, device model, graphics quality and frame rate, plus daily counts of screens, taps and in-game decisions. Used for live game statistics. Your "currently playing" record is removed when you leave the game; other records are kept for at most 90 days.
  • Weekly Event leaderboard (from version 2.2.0, Premium only; Firebase Realtime Database, Google, servers in the EU, europe-west1): when you play the Weekly Event, your result is saved to that event's leaderboard together with your Firebase user ID (a pseudonymous ID used as the record key), a display name (your Play Games name on Android or your Game Center name on iOS if you are signed in, otherwise an automatic name such as "Koordinatör 4821", shown in your language; you cannot type free text), the event scores (for example score, stability and legitimacy), the number of attempts, the time and the game version. Leaderboards are public: other players see the top 3 and their own rank in the game, and the records, including the user ID, can be read by anyone. They are kept for at most 90 days after the event ends, and you can ask us by email to delete yours (see section 7). The PC version and iOS versions before 2.2.0 do not send results.
  • Scenario Workshop submissions (optional; Firebase Realtime Database, Google, servers in the EU, europe-west1): when you send a scenario to us with "Send to HanX", we store the scenario itself (map setup, rules, win and loss conditions, and the title, short description and briefing text you wrote), the author name you typed if you allowed it to be shown (otherwise no name is sent), your anonymous Firebase user ID (the record key), the scenario's language, the game version, the time of sending and the permissions you gave. To prevent abuse, we also keep how many scenarios your account has sent and when it last sent one. Submissions are seen only by HanX Games; the game never shows them to other players. With the permission you give when sending, we may review your scenario, edit it if needed, translate it into 11 languages and publish it to all players as a Weekly Event; your name is shown only if you allowed it and after we have checked it, otherwise it reads "Anonymous Commander". Do not put personal information in scenario texts. Submissions that are not picked, and the submission counter, are kept for at most 180 days; a scenario published as an event stays in the event definition with its author's name (if allowed). You can ask us by email to delete your submission or your published scenario; tell us the scenario's title and roughly when you sent it (see section 7). The PC version does not send submissions.
  • Firebase Authentication and Google Play Games: an anonymous user ID; if you choose to sign in with Play Games, your Play Games player ID and display name, and your progress (achievements, career statistics, best results and campaign saves) is backed up to Play Games saved games in your Google account so you can continue on another device. On iOS, Game Center and iCloud play this role (section 1).
  • Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Messaging: settings delivered to the game and a push notification token, used for update notices and optional reminders. You can turn notifications off in the game's settings.
  • Google AdMob (free version only): the advertising ID and device information, used to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Google Play Billing: purchase records needed to unlock Premium. Payments are processed by Google.

This data is encrypted in transit, is not sold, and is used only for the purposes above. Google's processing (on both platforms) is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Children

ReBirth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Retention and your rights

We hold no data about you on our own servers. Data processed through Google services (AdMob on both platforms, Firebase on Android and on iOS from version 2.2.0) is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above; Weekly Event leaderboard records are deleted at most 90 days after the event ends. Scenario Workshop submissions that are not picked are deleted at most 180 days after they were sent. Cloud saves are kept in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account and can be deleted there; Game Center and iCloud data are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can ask us for access to or deletion of any data associated with you, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

5. This website

This website uses no cookies, ads or trackers and does not identify you. Your language and sound choices and your mini-game best scores are kept only in your browser's local storage. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its animation library from cdnjs (Cloudflare); these providers may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site.

To know how many people visit, the site counts page views as daily totals in our Firebase database (Google): the page, the page language, the kind of site you came from (for example "Google" or "Reddit", taken only from that site's domain name), the device type (phone or computer) and clicks on store links. No cookie, identifier or IP address is stored with these totals, and nothing is sent if your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on. Google may process technical data such as your IP address to receive the request.

Our Google Play links carry a campaign tag (for example utm_source=rebirthgame.tr) so that Google Play can show us, as a total, how many installs came from this site. The tag contains no personal data. The home page also shows itch.io's purchase box for the PC version; it loads from itch.io when you scroll to it, and from then on itch.io may process data such as your IP address and device information, and may store data on your device, under its own privacy policy (itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy).

Some news posts (for example, our call for community moderators) link to an application form on Google Forms. This site stores nothing; your answers go to our Google account (Google Forms and Sheets). We use them only to review your application and to reply to you, and we delete them within six months, or sooner if you ask.

Some news posts contain a YouTube video. Until you press play, the page shows only our own image and contacts no YouTube server. When you press play, the player loads from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); from then on Google may process data such as your IP address and device information, and may store data on your device, under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
