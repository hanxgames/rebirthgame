New maps · November 2, 2026

Two new maps in the campaign: Halvarya Fjords and Saranis Islands

News · ReBirth

In Halvarya, avalanches and snow load close the mountain roads; on Saranis, the volcano brings ash clouds and a giant wave. Now 5 maps, 5 disasters!

Two new maps have joined the ReBirth campaign! With update 2.1.4, Halvarya Fjords and Saranis Islands are waiting for you on the New campaign screen. That makes 5 maps and 5 disasters.

Halvarya Fjords: A northern coast carved by fjords, with pine forests and the glaciated Buzkırı Mountains. After the harshest winter in a century, avalanches buried the mountain towns.

  • Snow piles up in every district through the long winter; once the snow load passes its limit, avalanches come down, houses collapse and mountain roads stay closed for days.
  • The cold is an enemy too: in districts without heating, services falter and public support melts away.
  • New projects: Avalanche Barrier, Snow Clearing Station, Warming Center, Avalanche Monitoring Network (early warning), Geothermal Heating Network and more; new policy: Winter Mobilization Act.
  • New Snow map layer: see which districts are about to let go.

Saranis Islands: A peninsula of vineyards, a lagoon and volcanic islands. Morasca volcano is awake.

  • Volcanic pressure keeps rising; ash clouds drift on the wind, bury fields and vineyards and slow the economy.
  • An eruption can trigger a giant wave: coastal districts without a sea wall take heavy damage.
  • Islands without a ferry suffer weaker services and economy. Once the ash clears, the volcanic soil turns fertile, and the Terraced Vineyard Cooperative gets twice the benefit.
  • New projects: Breakwater and Sea Wall, Ash Clearing Crew, Volcano Observatory (evacuation warning), Ferry Line, Terraced Vineyard Cooperative and more; new policy: Coastal Planning Act. The new Ash map layer marks ash cover and unprotected coasts.

Each map has its own scenario, story, projects, policies and events, with 21 new events across the two maps.

Two new Play Games achievements: Shield of the North (protect Halvarya's five most avalanche-prone districts with an Avalanche Barrier) and Born from the Ashes (survive an eruption on Saranis by evacuating on the Volcano Observatory's warning).

2.1.4 is out now on Google Play and on PC (itch.io). Update the game and pick your map on the New campaign screen!
