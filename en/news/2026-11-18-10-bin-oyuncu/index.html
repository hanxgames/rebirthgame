10,000 players in 4 weeks: thank you all!

News · ReBirth

ReBirth passed 10,000 players in just four weeks! A heartfelt thank you to everyone who played, left a review and sent us ideas and bug reports.

ReBirth has passed 10,000 players in just four weeks! This is a huge milestone for us, and we owe it to you. Thank you all, from the bottom of our hearts.

We've come a long way together in these four weeks:

  • The campaign that began in Tarsel now spans five maps: Tarsel, Kesra, Varzan, Halvarya and Saranis.
  • You switched sides to lead the insurgency in Shadow Front and made the hardest calls at the Crisis Desk.
  • With Statecraft (Beta), you went from running a province to ruling an entire country.
  • You defended Arden in the Black Cholera event and competed on equal terms in the Weekly Events.
  • With Sandbox (Beta), you now build and share your own scenarios.
  • ReBirth now speaks 12 languages, with Russian the latest to join.

Much of this was shaped by you. Your store reviews, emails and suggestions steered every update, and the bugs you reported made the game a little sturdier every week.

The journey is only just beginning. Keep sending your feedback, ideas and bug reports to hanxdestek@gmail.com, or leave a review on Google Play or the App Store; we read every message.

See you on the campaign, commander!
