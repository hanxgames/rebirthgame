Version 2.2.6: Puppet states and the World Concord Assembly

News · ReBirth

In Statecraft, conquered lands get military, civil or autonomous rule, puppet states can be set up, and the World Concord Assembly meets in major crises.

2.2.6 is out now on Google Play! This update brings occupation rule, puppet states and a global assembly to Statecraft (Beta).

  • Occupation rule: govern conquered regions under military, civil or autonomous rule; each affects resistance, integration, income and prestige differently. Reconstruction, economic revival and resource projects.
  • Puppet states: at the peace table, or by re-establishing a country you annexed, set up a puppet government, a protectorate or a client state. Tribute, shared votes and wars; but if independence pressure builds up, rebellion.
  • World Concord Assembly: the continent's nations and four distant powers meet in major crises. Global ceasefires, sanctions and embargoes, peacekeepers and reconstruction funds; you can call an emergency session and lobby for votes.
  • Also: four new guide tips, three new military handbook pages, five new achievements; all 37 Statecraft achievements are now on Play Games. New English and Turkish voice lines, smarter AI (trade, defense industry, puppet management) and a foreign trade line in the monthly report.
