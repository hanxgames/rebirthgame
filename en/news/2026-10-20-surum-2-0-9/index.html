Version 2.0.9: Smoother, in 11 languages

News · ReBirth

Smoother play without sacrificing visuals, fewer stutters on effects, automatic graphics for your device, 5 new languages, and Google Play Games achievements, leaderboards and cloud save.

The feedback we heard most from players was that the frame rate stayed low on budget phones. That is the focus of 2.0.9: we made the game smoother without sacrificing visual quality.

  • Automatic graphics are now on for phones too: the game watches the frame rate and, if it stays below 30 FPS, lowers the quality by one step on its own (High → Medium → Low). If you pick a quality yourself in Settings, the automatic setting turns off
  • Faster without changing the picture: the terrain, tree and water shaders were streamlined; idle effects are no longer drawn; map labels, units and sky reflections are only recalculated when they change. Every step was compared pixel by pixel against the original image
  • On our test phone, the graphics card's work per frame dropped by 6.5% on Medium and 6% on Low. With automatic graphics, weak phones do 2.3 times less work: a phone that ran at 10 FPS on Medium now gets about 23 FPS
  • Fewer stutters: effects, night lights and the jet are now prepared while the map loads. The longest hitch in the first air strike dropped from 460 ms to 126 ms, and in the first night from 242 ms to 60 ms
  • 5 new languages: Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Indonesian. ReBirth is now available in 11 languages
  • Google Play Games: 32 achievements and two leaderboards (highest score and fastest victory)
  • Cloud save: your achievements, career stats and saves are stored in your Google Play Games account, so you can pick up where you left off when you install the game on a new phone
  • Like / dislike buttons on news cards: tell us what you think of an announcement with a single tap
  • Bug fixes and stability improvements, including a rare crash when returning to the main menu after voting in a poll and starting a campaign

Get the update on Google Play. We keep working on the next improvements; thank you for your feedback!
