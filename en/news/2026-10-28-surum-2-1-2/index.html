Version 2.1.2: A deeper campaign, shaped by players

News · ReBirth

An order wheel for units, growing consequences for budget deficits, clearer difficulty levels, combined scenarios and 5 strategies for the Shadow Front. The Story of the Campaign is rebuilt and reported bugs are fixed.

2.1.2 was shaped largely by suggestions from our players.

  • Order wheel: tap a unit and pick orders from the slices around it; choose which unit you mean when several overlap
  • Budget deficits no longer end the game at once: the bigger and longer the deficit, the heavier the interest, service cuts and loss of support
  • Political capital rebalanced: more of it early on, hoarding it costs you; new decisions for the late game
  • Easy, Medium and Hard now feel different: events, rebel behaviour and international reactions all scale with difficulty
  • Custom campaign rebuilt: Map, Scenario, Doctrine and Difficulty on separate tabs; combine scenarios into your own challenge
  • See why inflation moves: a forecast on the gauge and a detailed breakdown
  • 13 new events, rebalanced decisions, mid and late game pressures
  • New Healing map layer; hide the News panel on the main menu
  • The Story of the Campaign: readable pacing, pause, previous/next moment and 0.5×–2× speed
  • Shadow Front: 5 strategies, outpost raid plans, Heat and militant balance, new Council resolutions, more varied checkpoints
  • Crisis Desk: more time for stars on the Dam and Headquarters missions
  • Fixed: Shadow Front closing on restart, screen freezing after changing graphics settings, options that could be picked without enough political capital, Engineer Company build capacity

Thank you, Prempiti! Many of the fixes and changes in this update came from the detailed feedback of our player Prempiti. Thank you for taking the time to write to us.

The update is out now on PC (itch.io) and arrives on Google Play once store review is complete.
