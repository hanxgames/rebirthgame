New mode · October 22, 2026

See the story through the Shadow Front's eyes!

News · ReBirth

This time, you are the uprising. The new Shadow Front mode is out now in 2.1.1: lead the Council, win the people's trust and bring the mission to the peace table.

Watch the Shadow Front's opening cinematic above, made from the game's own scenes and voice acting.

After the earthquake the state fell apart, and a foreign mission now decides the province's fate. The Shadow Front is a movement of people who want to govern their own land: farmers, teachers, miners. For the first time in ReBirth, you are on the other side.

  • Lead the Council: organize your cells and weigh the cost of every decision
  • Win the people's trust: your strength comes from the people, and civilians are never a target
  • Get past gendarmerie checkpoints on the road: your answers decide the fate of your cell
  • Bring the mission to the peace table
  • Face a mission that knows the game: Easy, Medium and Hard, across three provinces
  • A dedicated interface and voice acting for the mode

The Shadow Front is out now in 2.1.1; get the update on Google Play.
