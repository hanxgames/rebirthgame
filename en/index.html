Five maps, five disasters

Every province has its own wound

Earthquake · MountainsFree

Tarsel Province

Aftershocks bring down unreinforced buildings. Watch the seismic network, set up tent cities and search and rescue, retrofit homes against the next quake.

Flood · DeltaPremium

Kesra Delta

The river rises every spring. Build levees, pumping stations and stilt houses, then farm the fertile silt the water leaves behind.

Drought · PlateauPremium

Varzan Plateau

Every district has its own water stress, and thirsty young men join the Shadow Front more easily. Drill wells, build cisterns, run tankers, plant a green belt.

Avalanche · FjordsPremium

Halvarya Fjords

Snow piles up in every district through the long winter; avalanches bring down homes and close mountain roads. Build avalanche barriers and a monitoring network, clear the roads, and hold off the cold with warming centers.

Volcano · IslandsPremium

Saranis Islands

Pressure inside Morasca volcano never stops rising. Ash buries the fields, and an eruption can send a giant wave into the coast. Build breakwaters and an observatory, run ferries to the islands, plant vineyards in the fertile volcanic soil.

FAQ

Good questions

Which devices can I play on?

Android phones and tablets (Google Play), iPhone and iPad (App Store) and PC: the Windows and Linux version and a free browser demo are on itch.io.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. ReBirth plays fully offline.

Is it paid?

The full Tarsel campaign is free. Premium is a one-time purchase with no subscription, and it also removes ads.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Voice acting in English and Turkish.

How long is a campaign?

A full campaign takes a few hours and picks up where you left off. Crisis Desk missions fit in a single session.

Is it like Rebel Inc.?

Same family: it blends rebuilding with counter-insurgency. ReBirth expands it with five disaster maps, a 3D world, an air force and crisis missions.
