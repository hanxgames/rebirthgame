What is Sandbox?

The Sandbox (Beta) button on the main menu opens the Scenario Workshop: a scenario editor for the campaign. You decide who holds each district, where units and insurgent cells stand, the rules and the victory condition, then play it and share it with friends.

Quick start: templates

While My Scenarios is empty, the template gallery opens. Pick a template and tap Start to begin editing from a ready-made scenario.

A scenario in four steps

  • Map: use brushes to give districts to the Government, mark them contested or hand them to the Shadow Front, and place units and cells. Tap a district to open its file and fine-tune control, headquarters, indicators, units, cells and ready-made buildings.
  • Rules: side, map, difficulty, doctrine, phase and the flow of time; civil, military, Shadow Front and event settings; reinforcements.
  • Objectives: add conditions to the You win and You lose cards, then set the duration and what happens when time runs out.
  • Name & share: title, short description, briefing and author name.

Test, save, play

Tap Test on the top bar to play your scenario at any moment, and Undo to roll back your latest edits. In play, an objective card tracks your progress and a results screen shows how your scenario ended.

Share with friends

Tap Copy code in the Name & share step or in My Scenarios: your scenario is copied to the clipboard as a single code. Send it to a friend; they paste it in the paste-code window in My Scenarios and play it in their own game. You can open their scenarios the same way.

Send to HanX

Send the scenario you're proud of with the Send to HanX button. Scenarios picked by our team are offered to every player as events, with your name on them if you wish. Our privacy policy explains how submissions are handled.

Community scenarios

We'll announce picked scenarios on this page and in the game's News section. The first one could be yours!

Tips for a good scenario

  • Start from a one-line idea: a lone fortress under siege in the capital, or the last city left in a country in revolt.
  • Keep objectives simple: players should see at a glance what they need to achieve.
  • Tell the story in the briefing: players should know why they are there.
  • Before sharing, play it once from start to finish with Test.
