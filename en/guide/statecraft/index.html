What is Statecraft?

Statecraft (Beta) is ReBirth's grand strategy mode. This time you don't run a single province but a whole country: for sixty years starting in 2031, on the continent of Aldorya.

To start, pick New game → Statecraft on the main menu and choose one of five countries: Tarsel, Kesra, Varzan, Halvarya or Saranis. Each has its own form of government, traditions and problems.

Know your country

Treasury, growth, debt, inflation, unemployment, stability, public support, parliamentary support, political capital, international reputation and technology are your country's pulse. A short report at the end of each month sums up what is getting better and what is getting worse.

Parliament, laws and the constitution

Balance the budget, pass your laws through Parliament and get ready for elections. You can break with your coalition partner; you see the cost and the partner's resentment in advance.

To change the constitution, set up a commission, pass it through Parliament or call a referendum. Countries' traditions can be reformed or abolished.

Research and the Path of Ascent

The research tree and the Path of Ascent shape your country's future across eras. Where you invest affects your economy, army and reputation even years later.

Economy, foreign trade and defense

  • Foreign trade: for every good you choose domestic production, whom to buy from and whom to sell to. Distant powers offer cheap goods but carry delivery risk.
  • Investment: expert missions, investment abroad and foreign investment incentives grow your economy.
  • Defense: you split the security budget between the armed forces, gendarmerie and intelligence; the defense industry grows with investment and can open up to arms exports.

Diplomacy and the Council of Continents

Sign agreements, table motions at the Council and pick a side in votes. Continental tension is out in the open; when crises escalate you can send arms and volunteers to your friends.

Council sanctions can turn into embargoes. Restricting or suspending trade with partners is also in your hands.

War and conquest

Command your armies on the map, plan your attack on the Offensive Operation screen and watch it unfold in real time.

Govern conquered land under military, civil or autonomous rule; each affects resistance, integration, income and reputation differently. At the peace table you can set up a puppet state. In major crises the World Concord Assembly meets to vote on global ceasefires, sanctions and peacekeepers.

Enter your country

See your country on the 3D map and watch investments, disasters and election rallies up close.

Tips

  • Don't let the deficit grow: debt and inflation slowly wear down stability.
  • Save political capital for big reforms.
  • Watch your relations with neighbors: tension and a bad reputation can leave you alone on the continent.
  • Think about the cost of conquest before you go to war: conquered land has to be governed.
